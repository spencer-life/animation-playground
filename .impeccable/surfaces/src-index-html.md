# Playground surface

Mode: Operate, with a gallery of educational motion examples.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A rack of motion samples that can be taken apart. The examples occupy the first viewport; there is no marketing hero before the work.

OWN-WORLD: Cool off-white canvas, dark graphite type, solid neutral shell selections, clear neutral control outlines, a cobalt accent for focus and small useful cues, and distinct muted lavender, apricot, blue, and green demo fields. Pinned Inter typography, generous sample stages, compact tools, and no ornamental gradients or glow. Demonstration palettes retain their own deliberate solid, outlined, and limited soft treatments.

STORY: Browse, try, inspect, take. Visitors can start with a project collection or everyday/expressive purpose, narrow by technology and complexity, and see finite previews play as their stages enter view. Every card offers Copy code, Details, and Replay beneath its metadata. Details opens the shared reference in place, beginning with How it works and retaining Code, Colors, Copy for AI, full-example copy, HTML download, and a palette-aware link. Closing returns to the same browsing position. The workbench remains available for direct interaction and speed controls. The selected project palette travels from the preview into the portable source.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim left category rail, compact utility header, a clear gallery title at 38px, and one sentence of context. Project collections and compact discovery controls lead into a three-column wall of working samples. Each sample has a 232px stage, a name and concise metadata beneath it, and a persistent row of solid Copy code, outlined Details, and outlined Replay actions with 44px targets. A shared native color disclosure previews Original, Radix, Geist-inspired, or custom palettes in Light, Dark, or Match site; it starts open on desktop and closed at widths of 780px or less so the phone view reaches its first study sooner. The native Details dialog keeps its heading and export footer around a scrolling reference, while the gallery stays in place and covered previews pause. The detail page pairs a large interactive stage with readable source, usage notes, and color integration. Mobile turns the rail and project collections into horizontal strips and shows one generous sample per row.
